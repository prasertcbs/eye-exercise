Not a medical device. This app reproduces protocols from published trials for general visual comfort. It does not diagnose anything, and no eye exercise corrects refractive error. Convergence and accommodative training target specific diagnosed conditions — if you have not been assessed, treat those two as optional. Stop and see an eye care professional for persistent double vision, eye pain, headache, new flashes or floaters, or any sudden change in vision.

Runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no network requests, no data leaves this device.

Before you start

These are exercise protocols copied from clinical trials, not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. They are aimed at visual comfort during screen work.

Exercises 3 and 4 (convergence and near–far focus) are therapies for diagnosed convergence insufficiency and accommodative dysfunction. If nobody has assessed your binocular vision, skip them or ask an optometrist first.

Stop immediately and get checked for persistent double vision, eye pain, headache, new flashes or floaters, or sudden vision change.
